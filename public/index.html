What does a scan check?

Every scan checks the address for known phishing reports, brand look-alikes, deceptive characters, suspicious URL patterns, and other signals. Each warning appears below the score with its category and explanation.

What happens during a live check?

Live inspection is optional and off by default. When you opt in, ClearSignal checks the public site's TLS certificate, redirects, DNS, domain age, and a limited amount of page HTML. It does not run the site's JavaScript.

What does the result mean?

Scores below 8 are labeled safe, 8–69 suspicious, and 70 or higher blocked by this app. These are heuristic signals, not certainty: sites can be missed or flagged by mistake.

What does “blocked” do?

High-scoring domains are added to ClearSignal's local blocklist and flagged in later scans. The app does not change your browser, operating system, or network settings. Hosts-file rules are only downloaded for your review.

Found a problem or a false alarm?

Report a bug or suggest an improvement on GitHub. Include what you expected and what happened; remove private URLs or personal information before sharing.
